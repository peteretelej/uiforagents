The paper-ledger statement

A light print-precision analytics identity: cool paper, near-black ink, one deep ink-blue accent, accounting double rules, and a mono tabular digit for every figure. Everything below is built from foundation.css alone - the screen document is the printout.

01 · design rationale

decisions, not options

paper-ledger exists so an agent building a cost report or a board pack stops making layout decisions and starts making statement decisions. The masthead, the three-part statement heading, the KPI row, the ledger tables, and the print stylesheet are fixed grammar. You choose the figures, the periods, and the words; the identity does the rest.

The signature is the rule hierarchy. Rules are arithmetic notation: a hairline separates rows, a strong rule bounds a block, a single accounting rule under a subtotal says the figures above sum to the figure below, and the double rule says the number is proved and nothing prints beneath it. Rules run under figure columns, never across labels - a rule under numbers is arithmetic, a rule across the row is a divider.

Precision is enforced typographically: every numeral is mono and tabular, so columns of figures form a hard right edge and digits align down the page. Negatives print in parentheses, zeros print as a dash, currency appears on the first figure of a column and on totals, units live in the column heads, and a statement that does not foot carries a rounding note. Color is state, never decoration: one ink-blue accent for the rubric voice, green/amber/red only where a figure or a badge genuinely changes meaning.

print decisions

1. folio and running foot set via @page margin box, counter(page) of counter(pages).

2. real thead elements: browsers repeat column heads on every continued page.

3. tr break-inside avoid: no row ever tears; subtotal and total rules travel with their figures.

4. headings bind forward (break-after avoid); orphans and widows held at 3.

5. the scheme pins light at the root for paper; print-color-adjust exact keeps the ink-blue and hatches.

6. ink lightens for paper: the line tokens soften one step inside the print block.

7. everything marked .pl-noprint drops out; the statement prints alone.
