The october board

A warm-light personal tracking identity: cream ground, terracotta accent, rounded bento cards, bold rounded numerals, and a progress grammar that reads goals, budgets, and streaks at a glance - with a voice that encourages. Everything below is built from foundation.css alone.

oct 09 2026 · light-dark() tokens, light lead · system stacks, rounded numerals · hand-authored svg charts · zero required javascript

01 · design rationale

Dashboards for one person read differently from consoles for teams. The ops identities score systems; the ledger scores documents. This one scores you against your own intentions - which takes warmth, roundness, and a state ladder with exactly two steps. A missed week is information, not an incident.

What makes it tally-board

  • Progress grammar - track = the period, fill = actual, target tick = today's pacing line. The same three parts render budgets, loops, and rings.
  • Two warm states - ok and warn. No red anywhere; the gentle retry pill handles the one error case a personal board has.
  • Rounded numerals - the display stack at weight 800 is the loudest voice on the page; no mono, ever.
  • Encouragement as grammar - every card gets a .tb-take reading; records get flagged once; copy names the next step.
  • Soft bento - the only identity with a soft shadow; cards float on cream instead of sitting in ruled sheets.

What stays out

  • Severity grammar - no danger token, no FAIL rows, no alarm lists. The census verdict keeps ops grammar out of this identity.
  • Audit density - tables stay small and friendly: hairlines, mini tracks, pills. Accounting rulings live in paper-ledger.
  • Mono figures - ledger tables and ops consoles own the mono voice; here numerals stay rounded and warm.
  • Shame - the flu week still landed 19 loops, and the board says so. Coaching copy never scolds.

02 · token reference

Every token is a light-dark() pair: light is the canonical lead, dark is a warm-dark derivative re-gated for contrast. Accent works as a split - --accent fills, --accent-deep carries text - and there is no danger token by design.

10 · print, modestly

Print is not this identity's signature (that honor belongs to a certain ledger), so the print section stays modest: the light scheme pins at the root, card shadows drop for hairline borders, the chrome and controls hide, cards and rows never tear, and a warm-gray folio rides the @page margin box. Print this page: sections 03-08 are the board, and the fridge is the deployment target.

What print changes

  • light re-inks every token for paper - nothing is hand-recolored
  • shadows drop; a 1px warm border keeps the bento readable on white
  • the period switcher, buttons row, and this explanation chrome hide
  • cards, stats, timeline and goal rows, and table rows never split across pages
  • the loading pulse dies; heat cells and ring arcs keep their fills
